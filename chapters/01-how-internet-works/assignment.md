# Assignment: Explain the Internet (Unpaid)

## 📝 Assignment Overview

Write a blog post in your own words explaining key internet concepts. This assignment will help solidify your understanding and demonstrate your ability to communicate technical concepts clearly.

## 📋 Requirements

Write a blog post that answers the following questions:

1. **How does the internet work?**
   - Explain in simple terms that a non-technical person could understand
   - Include key concepts like packets, routers, and protocols

2. **What are the most important characteristics of the internet and what's their purpose?**
   - Discuss concepts like decentralization, scalability, resilience
   - Explain why these characteristics matter

3. **What is HTTP? What is HTTPS?**
   - Explain the difference between the two
   - Why is HTTPS important for security?

## ✍️ Writing Guidelines

- **Length:** 500-800 words
- **Style:** Clear, concise, and accessible
- **Originality:** Use your own words (no copy-paste from sources)
- **Examples:** Include real-world examples where appropriate
- **Format:** Use headings, bullet points, and formatting for readability

## 📤 Submission Process

1. **Create an account** on [Medium.com](https://medium.com)
2. **Write your blog post** as a draft
3. **Submit to your mentor** for review
4. **Incorporate feedback** and make revisions
5. **Get mentor approval**
6. **Publish the article** on Medium
7. **Share the link** on the CodeBlossom Slack channel

## ✅ Evaluation Criteria

Your blog post will be evaluated on:

- **Understanding:** Do you demonstrate a clear grasp of the concepts?
- **Clarity:** Is your explanation easy to follow?
- **Accuracy:** Are your technical details correct?
- **Originality:** Did you write in your own words?
- **Engagement:** Is the post interesting and well-structured?

## 💡 Tips for Success

- Read your post out loud to check for clarity
- Have a friend or family member read it - can they understand it?
- Use analogies to explain complex concepts
- Include diagrams or images if helpful
- Proofread for grammar and spelling

## ⏰ Deadline

Submit your draft to your mentor within **3 days** of completing the chapter materials.
